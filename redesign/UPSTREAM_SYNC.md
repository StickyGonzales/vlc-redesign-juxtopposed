# Upstream sync (branch wip/upstream-sync)

Cone brought up to date with upstream VLC master: a merge of `914c465b0c` (VideoLAN's master of 2026-11-13) into `wip/next-test`. Cone's fork point was `a5538c557d` (2026-10-12); upstream added **398 commits** since, Cone **1402**. Not for 1.5.0 (StickyGonzales, 2026-11-13).

## What upstream brought in
